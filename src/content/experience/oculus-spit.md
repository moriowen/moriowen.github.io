## Running the tech for a fest

As Tech Head, I built and shipped the public registration portal, a 3D campus visualization,
and the dashboard used by event teams. The registration workflow connected the public site
with payment processing and gave organizers a way to monitor and update registrations.

## OC-Main

The public portal introduced online event registration through Cashfree. It reached more than
1,000 distinct users, handled over 500 registrations across 11 events, and recorded more than
Rs 65,000 in payments. Users and registrations are separate counts: a visitor could use the
site without registering for an event.

## OC-Model

I hosted and integrated a Three.js model of the campus for interactive navigation alongside
the festival website.

## OC-Admin

I created an admin dashboard where 23 non-technical event teams could monitor registration
status and modify records when needed, with updates synced through Firebase Firestore. I kept
the live dashboard private because it contained participant details.
