## Scope

Not a new system. The paper explains how autonomous vehicles perceive their environment, reviews
seven studies, summarizes the common computer-vision methods, covers safety and accident detection,
and lists deployment challenges.

## The central claim

Cameras and computer vision matter but are not sufficient on their own. Reliable autonomy needs
perception fused with LiDAR, radar, ultrasonic sensing, localization, mapping, planning, and
vehicle control.
